I. INTERNATIONAL AGREEMENTS

Basel Convention on the Control of Transboundary Movements of Hazardous Wastes

GLOBAL COVERAGE

Addresses the transboundary movements of hazardous wastes and their disposal, including plastic waste amendments adopted in 2019.

Adopted: 1989 Effective: 1992 Parties: 191 countries
Official Text Signatory List

Bamako Convention on Hazardous Wastes within Africa

REGIONAL (AFRICA FOCUSED)

Prohibits the import of hazardous waste into Africa and regulates the transboundary movement and management of hazardous wastes produced within the continent.

Adopted: 1991 Effective: 1998 Parties: 30 African Union member states
Official Text

Stockholm Convention on Persistent Organic Pollutants

GLOBAL COVERAGE

Addresses persistent organic pollutants (POPs) including those found in waste streams such as pesticides, industrial chemicals, and unintentional by-products.

Adopted: 2001 Effective: 2004 Parties: 185 countries
Official Text

II. REGIONAL FRAMEWORKS

Africa Circular Economy Alliance (ACEA)

CONTINENTAL ALLIANCE

Multi-stakeholder platform advancing circular economy principles across Africa. Founding members include the African Union, World Economic Forum, African Development Bank, World Bank, and South Africa (2022).

Established: 2017 (Relaunched 2022) Scope: Continental policy harmonization

African Union Agenda 2063

CONTINENTAL FRAMEWORK

Africa's strategic framework for socio-economic transformation, including environmental sustainability goals and waste management as part of Aspiration 1 (Prosperous Africa based on inclusive growth and sustainable development).

Adopted: 2015 Timeline: 2013-2063

East African Community (EAC) Protocol on Environment and Natural Resources

REGIONAL (EAST AFRICA)

Harmonizes environmental management policies among EAC partner states (Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan, DRC), including waste management standards and transboundary waste movement.

Effective: 2006 Members: 7 partner states

ECOWAS Policy on Environment

REGIONAL (WEST AFRICA)

Economic Community of West African States environmental policy framework addressing waste management, pollution control, and environmental protection across 15 West African nations including Nigeria.

Adopted: 2008 Members: 15 West African states
