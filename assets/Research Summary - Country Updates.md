## **SOUTH AFRICA** 🇿🇦

### Key Statistics & Updates (2024-2026)
- **Draft National Waste Management Strategy 2026** published December 19, 2025
- **37% of households** lack access to refuse removal services
- **29% of plastic waste** not collected (≈440,000 tonnes leaking into environment annually)
- **Material cycling rate: 7%** across all materials (5% recycling, 2% reuse)
- **2 kg waste/person/day** generation rate
- **Ranked 11th globally** for mismanaged plastic waste entering marine environment
- Waste management market: **$280M (2020) → projected $330M (2025)**
- **EPR regulations** effective since May 2021 - mandatory for e-waste, paper, and plastics packaging
- **African Circular Economy Alliance (ACEA)** founding member since 2017

### Major Initiatives
- **Circular South Africa (CSA)** - national circular economy stakeholder platform led by ACEN
- **National Circular Economy Action Plan** development underway (24-month timeline)
- **Just Transition** policies integrating informal waste pickers with compensation & registration
- **Separation-at-source** programs expanding nationwide
-30-40% of domestic solid waste is organic, 10-20% paper and packaging

### Leading Innovations
- **IoT sensors and smart bins** optimizing collection routes (30% cost reduction)
- **Kibo Gauteng Thermal Power Station** - pyrolysis for plastic-to-energy
- **Regenize** - mobile recycling incentives platform
- **Boomba.mobi** - waste-for-food exchange system with worm farms

### Policy Framework
- **National Environmental Management: Waste Act (NEMWA) 2008**
- **National Waste Management Strategy 2020 & Draft 2026**
- **EPR Regulations 2021** - producers must register with PROs
- **Waste Classification & Management Regulations**

## **NIGERIA** 🇳🇬

### Key Statistics & Updates (2024-2026)
- **Lagos generates 13,000 tons/day** - 60% uncollected, only 13% recycled
- **National: 32 million metric tons/year** → projected **107 million tons by 2050**
- **20-30% national collection rate** (41% in optimized urban areas like Lagos)
- **0.51 kg/day per capita** waste generation
- **1.5 million tonnes plastic waste/year** - <10% recycling rate
- **Lagos contributed 870,000 tons** to world's 57 million tons plastic waste (2024)
- **34 kg plastic/person/year** leaking into Lagos water system
- **Informal waste pickers handle 59.3%** of national waste disposal

### Major Initiatives (2025-2026)
- **Single-use plastic ban in Lagos State** (effective July 1, 2025) - styrofoam, straws, cutlery
- **Lagos-World Bank collaboration** - "Improving Solid Waste and Plastics Management" roadmap
- **National Solid Waste Sector Innovation Fund** launched
- **Phoenix Edison WtE facility** - USD 116M, processing 1,000+ tonnes/day
- **Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs)** expansion targeting 60% uncollected waste

### National Roadmap 2025-2040
-**2025 Quick Wins:** Launch innovation fund, boost at-source separation
- **2027 Short-term:** Improve collection infrastructure
- **2030 Medium-term:** Specialized hazardous waste facilities in key cities
- **2040 Long-term:** Integrated sustainable waste management system

### Leading Innovations
- **Wecyclers** - cargo bikes + SMS points system (diverted 5,000+ tonnes)
- **Vicfold Recyclers** - plastic-to-resin manufacturing
- **Salubata** - plastic waste-to-modular footwear
- **Taka Taka partnership** - 90% recycling targets in poor communities

### Policy Framework
- **Climate Change Act 2021** - Net-zero by 2060
- **National Policy on Plastic Waste Management (NPPWM)**
- **National Implementation Guidelines for Plastic Waste 2024** (UNIDO-Japan)
- **Gas-to-Waste Policy 2022** - methane capture & WtE
- **NESREA Act 2007** - main enforcement agency

## **KENYA** 🇰🇪

### Key Statistics & Updates (2024-2026)
- **Nairobi generates 2,400 tons/day** - projected to rise with population
- **Konza Technopolis pneumatic system** - KES 1.165 billion (€8M/USD 9M) - 2025
- **15 km underground pipeline network**, 100 waste inlets, **40 tons/day capacity**
- Serves **30,000 residents** at Konza
- **Eliminates 89 tonnes CO2 emissions annually** (no waste trucks needed)
- Waste travels at **70 km/hour** through vacuum pipes
- **4-way source separation:** plastics, paper, mixed waste, organics

### Major Projects (2025-2026)
- **Konza Technopolis** - Africa's first pneumatic waste collection (operational 2025)
- **Dandora WtE Plant** - 45 MW capacity, development agreement with China National Electric Engineering Co. (CNEEC)
- **Ruai WtE Plant** - Under construction as part of Kenya Vision 2030
- **Green Nairobi Company Limited** - new autonomous waste management body
- **4,000 Green Army workers** hired on permanent, pensionable terms (first since 1987)
- **27 tippers, 24 compactors, skip loaders** acquired by Nairobi County (2026)

### Dandora Dumpsite Transformation
- **President Ruto announcement (Feb 8, 2026):** Dandora relocation project
- **National + County joint financing** - over KSh 4 billion committed
- Waste treatment plant - **target completion 2027**
- Garbage collection from estates begins **April 1, 2026**
- Converts refuse to **fertilizer and energy**

### Leading Innovations
- **Konza pneumatic system** - 100 sensor-activated inlets, automated valves
- **HyaPak** - water hyacinth to biodegradable packaging
- **EcoPost** - plastic-to-construction materials (eco-fencing)
- **Mr. Green Africa** - tech-enabled plastic recycling platform

### Policy Framework
- **Sustainable Waste Management Act 2022**
- **Sustainable Waste Management Regulations 2024** - EPR compliance, licensing
- **EPR Regulations 2020** - Extended Producer Responsibility
- **Construction Waste Strategy 2025-2030** (National Construction Authority)
- **Plastic & single-use plastic bans** - strict criminal penalties for non-compliance

## **EGYPT** 🇪🇬

### Key Statistics & Updates (2024-2026)
- **Cairo generates 20,000 tons waste/day**
- **Zabbaleen collect 60%** of Cairo's waste, **recycle 80%** of collected waste
- **Zabbaleen daily processing:** 11,000 tons (Manshiyat Naser: 5,000 tons alone)
- **100,000 residents** of Manshiyat Naser ("Garbage City") live off waste activities
- **Recycling rate: 37% (2024)** → **Target: 60% by 2027** (from 10% in 2018)
- **Plastic recycling market:** USD 380.25M (2024) → projected USD 473.96M (2030)
- **27 confirmed e-waste facilities** (2024) - up from 6 previously
- **184 formal e-waste workers** (2024) - up from 99

### Major Projects (2025-2027)
- **Zero Carbon Green Planet (ZCGP)** - Cairo's 15 May landfill
  - **400 tonnes/day organic waste** → graphene, hydrogen, fertilizer
  - Phase 1 operational, full completion **2027**
  - Plans to scale to **10,000 tonnes/day**
- **Assiut Recycling Plant** - EGP 250M (USD 5.12M)
  - German-Dutch technology
  - **60 tons/hour capacity**
  - Completion **2026**
- **10th of Ramadan Integrated Waste Management City** - Middle East's first (inaugurated Feb 2024)

### Zabbaleen System
- **85% recycling efficiency** - world's most efficient informal system
- No cost to city - income from reselling recycled materials
- Products: quilts, rugs, paper, livestock food, compost, recycled plastics
- **Zero unemployment** in Manshiyat Naser neighborhood
- Ton of plastic sells for **$860**
- **15,000 tonnes/day** harvested and hand-sorted

### Leading Innovations
- **ZCGP plant** - organic waste to advanced materials (graphene, hydrogen)
- **Digital platforms** linking households, businesses collectors, recyclers
- **EGWM (Eco Green Waste Management) NGO** - represents 27+ formal recyclers
- **E-waste vocational training curricula** in schools (introduced 2024)
- **6 new e-waste management SMEs** from incubator programs

### Policy Framework
- **Law No. 202 of 2020** - Waste Management Regulation Law
- **Executive Regulations 2022** - licensing for public/private sectors
- **EPR for plastics & electronics** - producers finance collection/recycling
- **Prime Ministerial Decree No. 662 of 2025** - implementing EPR, plastic bag fees
- **National Solid Waste Management Programme (NSWMP)** - institutional overhaul
- **EPAP III** - Egypt Pollution Abatement Project

## **RWANDA** 🇷🇼

### Key Statistics & Updates (Already Documented)
- **1.5 million tonnes solid waste/year** - 75% biodegradable
- **<4% recycling rate** nationally
- **SHINE Model** piloted in 3 satellite cities: Bugesera, Rwamagana, Muhanga (2024-2025)
- **Enviroserve Rwanda:** 413 green jobs created, 70 youth trained
- **1,500 tonnes plastic** collected through Plastic Waste Management Fund
- **1,300 green jobs** created from plastic waste programs
- **EPR targets:** 30% (2024) → 50% (2026) → 70% (2028) → 100% (2030)
- **~2,500 electronics producers** managed by PRO

### Major Initiatives (2024-2026)
- **SHINE Model** - GIZ-developed sustainable waste management framework
- **National Circular Economy Action Plan** - zero-waste cities by 2035
- **Enviroserve Rwanda Green Park** - state-of-the-art e-waste facility
- **Plastic roads** in Kigali - durable, cost-effective alternatives
- **Quality composting facilities** - RSB standard compost production
- **Rwanda BioSolutions** - waste-to-compost social enterprise
