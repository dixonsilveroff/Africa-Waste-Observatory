Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit the Africa Waste Observatory.

Effective Date: February 13, 2026

1. Introduction

The Africa Waste Observatory is a publicly accessible, educational knowledge platform maintained by researchers at the Faculty of Engineering and Technology, Alex Ekwueme Federal University, Ndufu-Alike, Ikwo, Nigeria. This website provides data, analysis, and insights on waste management across the African continent.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you access and interact with our website. We are committed to transparency and to respecting your privacy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Analytics Data (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID: G-QVHZ3Q6F0J) to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics may collect:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website or source that brought you to our site
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level, derived from IP address)
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Screen resolution and language preferences

This data is aggregated and anonymized. We do not use Google Analytics to identify individual visitors. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

2.2 Local Storage

We use your browser's localStorage to save your language preference (English or French) so that it persists between visits. This data:

  • Is stored only on your device
  • Is never transmitted to our servers or any third party
  • Can be cleared at any time through your browser settings

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

This website does not:

  • Require user registration or account creation
  • Collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Use contact forms or comment systems
  • Process payments or financial transactions
  • Track you across other websites

3. Cookies

This website itself does not set cookies. However, Google Analytics may set cookies (such as _ga and _gid) in your browser to distinguish unique visitors and throttle request rates. These are first-party cookies managed by Google's analytics script.

You can control or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking these cookies will not affect the core functionality of the website — all content will remain fully accessible.

4. How We Use Information

The aggregated analytics data we receive is used solely to:

  • Understand which content is most useful to visitors
  • Identify opportunities to improve the website's structure and navigation
  • Monitor general traffic patterns and geographic reach
  • Support academic research on knowledge dissemination about waste management in Africa

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for commercial purposes.

6. Data Retention

Google Analytics data retention is configured according to Google's default settings (typically 26 months). After this period, aggregated analytics data is automatically deleted. Your language preference stored in localStorage persists until you clear your browser data.

7. Children's Privacy

The Africa Waste Observatory is an educational resource suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other visitors. Since no personal data is collected through this website, no special provisions for children's data are required.

8. Your Rights

As a visitor to this website, you have the right to:

  • Opt out of Google Analytics — Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Block cookies — Configure your browser to block third-party cookies or all cookies
  • Clear local storage — Remove your saved language preference through your browser's developer tools or settings
  • Request information — Contact us to ask what aggregated data, if any, relates to your usage

9. External Links

Our website contains links to external resources, references, and innovation websites. These links open in new tabs and are provided for informational purposes. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external site you visit.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
